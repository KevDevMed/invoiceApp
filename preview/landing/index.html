InvoiceApp — Invoices

Features

Fully offline

Everything is stored in one SQLite file on your Mac. No account to create, no server to talk to, no sync and no telemetry.

On-device assistant

A bundled llama.cpp runtime answers questions about your books. Weights download once, then every prompt is processed locally.

PDF invoices & reports

Build invoices from your client list, export them to PDF, and read back revenue and status reports without leaving the app.

Privacy by construction

Your data never
leaves the machine.

One local file
Clients, invoices and settings live in a single SQLite database in your macOS user data folder. To back it up, quit the app first, then copy the .db file along with its -wal and -shm siblings.
No account, no server, no telemetry
Nothing to sign up for and nothing phoning home. The app works with the network turned off.
Assistant runs on-device
Model weights are downloaded once, then llama.cpp runs them locally. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
Requirements
macOS 12 Monterey or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. Around 8 GB free disk space if you want the assistant.
Free and MIT-licensed
No price, no tiers, no upsell. Read or fork the source at github.com/KevDevMed/invoiceApp.
Built with
Electron, React, TypeScript and SQLite, with node-llama-cpp for the assistant.
